Digital design · FPGA

Traffic-Light Controller

A synchronous traffic-light controller in Verilog, implemented on an FPGA. I designed it as a finite-state machine, compared Mealy and Moore versions, and wrote testbenches to check every state transition and timing condition in simulation before running it on the board.

A few more things about me

Why a Music minor?

Music and signal processing are closer than they look. Audio is where I first got curious about filters and frequency response, and my work in the Music Cognition Lab keeps me thinking about how people actually hear signals.

What do you do for fun?

Video editing is my most consistent hobby: tweaking velocity, effects, and audio until a clip feels right. I'm also a big Star Wars fan, and I like channeling that into the things I design and build.

What are you looking for next?
